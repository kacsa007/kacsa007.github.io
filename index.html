How I think about engineering

Good engineering is not just shipping a service. It is understanding its full lifecycle: design, instrumentation, capacity, operations and the feedback loops in between. I lead teams and build software with that whole picture in mind — using automation and machine learning where they solve real operational problems, not as decoration.
